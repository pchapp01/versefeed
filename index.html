Thumb Bible

Scroll Scripture, not social.

Every day your thumb scrolls on autopilot. Thumb Bible points that habit at the Bible, one verse per swipe.

Swipe through the whole Bible in a calm, full-screen feed. Read the King James Version or the Bible in Basic English offline, keep a verse of the day, follow reading plans, and save the verses you love.

Thumb Bible Premium adds study aids for any verse: plain-English explanations, the original Hebrew, Greek and Aramaic, deeper context and related passages. Study aids are written with AI and clearly labelled.
