Restore a purchase

Open Verse Feed, go to Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Verse Feed Premium. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

You never need an account to read. If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account and synced data in Verse Feed: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This doesn't cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that separately as described above.

About the study aids

Explanations, original-language notes and related verses are written with AI. They can contain mistakes, so please weigh them against Scripture and trusted teachers. If you spot something wrong, email us the verse and the study type.

Translations

The King James Version and the Bible in Basic English are included and work offline. More translations will be added as licences are confirmed.
